This vest is one of a kind — an open-back silhouette hand-made from atlas ikat silk in a bold black-and-white pattern, finished with beaded accents along the neckline and a fringe hem that moves when you do.

Because the back stays open rather than closing over a full lining, the beading and fringe carry more of the visual weight than the fabric alone — it's built to be seen from behind as much as the front.

Cut from one run of handwoven yardage, the exact placement of the dye pattern across this piece won't be repeated in another vest.
