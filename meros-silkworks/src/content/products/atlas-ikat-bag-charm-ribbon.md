This one goes by Guli. It arrives tied as a bow — a length of atlas, the silk-based material better known as ikat, sourced from Uzbekistan and finished with a clip so it clasps onto a bag strap in seconds. Untie it and the same ribbon restyles into whatever shape suits the day; it isn't locked into being a bow.

It's become one of the shop's bestsellers, with a steady run of favorites and reviews calling out the color and the fact that you can reshape it yourself instead of being stuck with one look.

It's a small gift, an easy self-buy, and a good first piece if you're not ready to commit to a full garment yet.
