Doppi. Doppu. Doppa. — the name shifts by region, but the square cap it names is recognized as a symbol of Turkic identity across the world. This hair tie takes that shape and rebuilds it at a wearable scale: a 2in x 2in square, hand-made in Uzbekistan from atlas ikat.

It's a literal piece of the cap rather than a print referencing it — small enough to wear every day, but built from the same textile lineage as the rest of the shop.

Wear one stacked with the others, gift a set to someone who'll recognize what it is, or keep a single one on your wrist as a small, portable piece of where the pattern comes from.
