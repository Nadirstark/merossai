This is modern cultural wear built for a hat rack: atlas — the ikat silk running through the rest of the shop — wrapped around a structured base and finished with a second pass of hand-applied sequins and beading.

It comes in two silhouettes: a bucket hat and a baseball cap. Same embellished-atlas treatment, two different shapes — a brim you can pull low, or a bill you can wear forward, whichever fits how you actually wear a hat.

The embellishment goes on after the hat is built, so its placement against the underlying dye pattern is particular to that piece — it reads as a finished statement, not an afterthought.
