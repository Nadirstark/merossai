The baska is a fitted basque-style skirt, traditionally paired with an Uzbek wedding or event outfit, and every one made here starts as a custom order rather than a piece pulled off a shelf. There's no standing listing for it because there's no standard size or fabric run to stock — each baska is cut to the person wearing it.

That means there isn't a live Etsy listing to click through to for this piece. Instead, [start a custom request](/custom) with your measurements, the event date, and a sense of which atlas ikat colorway you're drawn to, and the details get worked out from there.

Past requests for this piece have mostly come from weddings and family events, where a fitted, made-to-order silhouette in a fabric with real family or cultural meaning matters more than picking something off the shelf.
